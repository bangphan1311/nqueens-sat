\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\caption{Size of a single at-most-one (AMO) encoding on a line of $k$ literals.
Exact formulas follow the implementation; asymptotic commander expressions are not exact counts.}
\label{tab:amo-blocks}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Encoding & Auxiliary variables & Clauses \\
\midrule
Pairwise
  & $0$
  & $\binom{k}{2}$ \\
Sequential counter ($k\ge 2$)
  & $k-1$
  & $3k-4$ \\
Binary (bitwise), $k=2$
  & $0$
  & $1$ \\
Binary (bitwise), $k\ge 3$
  & $\lceil\log_2 k\rceil$
  & $k\lceil\log_2 k\rceil$ \\
Commander
  & $\frac{k}{2}+O(\log k)$
  & $\frac{7k}{2}+O(\log k)$ \\
Product ($k\ge 2$)
  & $p+q$
  & $\binom{p}{2}+\binom{q}{2}+2k$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\vspace{2pt}
\begin{minipage}{0.96\linewidth}
\footnotesize
For $k\le 1$, all five implementations generate zero auxiliary variables and zero AMO clauses. For the product encoding, $p=\lceil\sqrt{k}\rceil$ and $q=\lceil k/p\rceil$. The commander expressions are asymptotic bounds for the recursive implementation with group size three.
\end{minipage}
\end{table}

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\caption{Size of the complete N-Queens CNF. The model contains $N^2$ board variables,
AMO constraints on rows, columns and both diagonal families, and $2N$ at-least-one (ALO) clauses.}
\label{tab:cnf-size}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Encoding & Auxiliary variables & Total clauses \\
\midrule
Pairwise
  & $0$
  & $\frac{5}{3}N^3-2N^2+\frac{7}{3}N$ \\
Sequential counter ($N\ge 2$)
  & $4N^2-6N+2$
  & $12N^2-22N+12$ \\
Binary (bitwise)
  & $\Theta(N\log N)$
  & $\Theta(N^2\log N)$ \\
Commander
  & $2N^2+O(N\log N)$
  & $14N^2+O(N\log N)$ \\
Product
  & $\Theta(N^{3/2})$
  & $12N^2+O(N^{3/2})$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\vspace{2pt}
\begin{minipage}{0.96\linewidth}
\footnotesize
The pairwise clause expression is exact for this encoding, including the $2N$ ALO clauses. The sequential-counter expressions are exact for $N\ge2$. The remaining rows state asymptotic growth; lower-order terms depend on line lengths and implementation details. These are encoding-size bounds, not runtime predictions.
\end{minipage}
\end{table}
